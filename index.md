# Just Now

A personal Experience Sampling app for iPhone. Catch yourself in the moment, then look back to find what drives your mood.

Coming to the App Store.

## What it does

- **Random buzzes** inside the windows you choose ask how you feel right now. One tap on a mood saves the moment; a note, activities, people, and a photo are optional.
- **Context attaches itself.** Time, place, and weather are captured with each moment, and you confirm or correct them before saving.
- **Look back** over a chosen period: a map of where your moments happened, mood over time, and breakdowns by place, activity, people, time of day, and weather.
- **Do Not Disturb** pauses the buzzes with one tap. Logging still works.
- **Your data stays yours.** Everything lives on your device. Deleted items sit in a recycle bin for 30 days.

Built on the [Experience Sampling Method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Experience_sampling_method): sampling random moments catches genuine experience before the mind rationalises it. The app shows correlation, not cause.

## Privacy

- No accounts
- No tracking
- No analytics
- No servers of our own
- No data leaves your device, except a weather lookup you can turn off by declining location
